# Bingo 251 — brand & marketing prompts

Everything below is written for a text-to-image model (Midjourney, Ideogram, Flux,
DALL·E, Firefly). Copy a prompt block as-is; replace the `{{…}}` placeholders when you
need a different headline.

---

## 1. Brand foundation

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#050D0B` | Backgrounds, app chrome |
| Panel | `#0B1A15` | Cards |
| Raised | `#10241D` | Inputs, hover |
| Gold | `#F3C969` | Primary brand, numbers, CTAs on dark |
| Deep gold | `#D9A63C` | Gold shading |
| Mint | `#37E0A0` | Success, live indicators, secondary CTA |
| Off-white | `#EEF6F1` | Headlines on dark |
| Muted | `#8AA79A` | Secondary text |
| Danger | `#FF6B6B` | Alerts only |

- **Mark:** a gold bingo ball containing “251”, the Ethiopian country code, inside a mint
  ring on a near-black rounded square. Read it as *a ball*, not a coin.
- **Feel:** modern Ethiopian nightlife — warm gold light, deep green-black, mint signal
  accents. Clean flat vector with a soft metallic sheen, never glossy 3D.
- **Motifs:** woven *tibeb* border strips (geometric Habesha textile), bingo balls,
  perforated card grids, concentric “drawn ball” rings, subtle Addis Ababa skyline.
- **Typography:** bold rounded display face for headlines (Bricolage Grotesque, Offside,
  Archivo Black), Inter for body. Amharic set in Noto Sans Ethiopic, same weight.
- **Copy voice:** short, confident, bilingual EN/AM. “Bingo 251”, “Play with credits”,
  “Powered by Ethiopia”. Always include an 18+ mark and “Play responsibly”.

Never: real bank notes, currency symbols (Birr/USD), religious crosses, the Ethiopian
flag as a background, photorealistic cash, or anything that reads as a sportsbook.

## 2. Logo generation

### 2.1 Primary app icon (1:1, 1024×1024)

> Flat vector app icon on a near-black rounded square (#050D0B, 22% corner radius).
> Centered: a circular bingo ball in warm metallic gold (#F3C969, subtle vertical
> gradient to #D9A63C) with a thin mint (#37E0A0) outer ring at 35% opacity. Inside the
> ball, a dark circle (#050D0B) with a 1px mint ring, and the numerals “251” in bold gold
> geometric sans, optically centered. Small white specular highlight on the upper-left of
> the ball. Clean edges, no text outside the mark, no drop shadow, no 3D render, no
> beveled glass, perfectly centered, generous safe margin (18% of canvas).

Negative prompt: `photograph, 3D render, glossy plastic, chrome, coin, roulette wheel,
casino chips, dollar sign, Birr symbol, gradient noise, text outside the circle, busy
details, drop shadow, watermark, mockup, multiple logos`.

### 2.2 Horizontal wordmark (16:9, 2048×1152, transparent)

> Minimal horizontal lockup: on the left the gold “251” bingo-ball mark described above,
> on the right the wordmark “Bingo” in off-white (#EEF6F1) bold rounded sans with the
> “251” in gold (#F3C969). Tight optical tracking, baseline aligned with the ball, flat
> vector, transparent background, no tagline, no border.

### 2.3 Monochrome / one-color variant

> Single-color version of the same lockup in pure white on transparent, and a matching
> one in #050D0B on transparent. No gradients, no highlights, identical geometry, safe
> for embossing, embroidery and 16px favicon use.

## 3. Poster system

Shared art direction for all posters:

> Premium flat-vector illustration with a subtle grain, deep ink-green background
> (#050D0B), warm gold key light from the top-right, mint accent glows. Woven tibeb
> border running along the top and bottom edges in gold on dark. Bingo balls float in
> the composition with motion-blur arcs. Ethiopian faces and hands when people appear —
> warm brown skin tones, modern clothing, joyful but not manic. Text-safe area in the
> upper third and along the bottom 20%. Keep the center clear for the headline.

### 3.1 Launch / hero poster (4:5 feed 1080×1350 and 9:16 story 1080×1920)

> Hero poster for “Bingo 251”. A large gold bingo ball marked “251” exploding into a
> swirl of smaller numbered balls above a glowing card grid, on deep ink-green. Behind
> it, a soft silhouette of the Addis Ababa skyline with mint window lights. A woven gold
> tibeb strip frames the top and bottom. Headline space at the top: “BINGO 251” in bold
> off-white with “251” in gold. Bottom space: “Play live bingo in Telegram” in mint, and a
> small 18+ badge. Cinematic rim light, generous negative space, flat vector with subtle
> grain, no photorealism.

Headline variants (EN / AM):
- `Bingo 251 — Live bingo, made in Ethiopia` / `ቢንጎ 251 — በኢትዮጵያ የተሰራ ቀጥታ ቢንጎ`
- `One ball. 251 ways to win.` / `አንድ ኳስ። 251 የማሸነፊያ መንገዶች።`

### 3.2 Deposit bonus poster (1:1 and 9:16)

> Promotional poster: a stack of glowing gold bingo balls rising out of a dark wallet
> card, mint light spilling from the card slot. Woven tibeb border in gold along the top.
> Large empty headline area at the top; bottom third shows a gold CTA pill outline and an
> 18+ mark. Color palette limited to ink green, gold, mint and off-white. Flat vector,
> soft bloom, no cash, no banknotes, no currency symbols. Mood: reward, not gambling
> chaos.

Headline: `Deposit. Play. Win.` / `ያስገቡ። ይጫወቱ። ያሸንፉ።`
Subline: “Bonus credits with clear wagering — see terms in the app.”

### 3.3 Referral / invite poster (9:16)

> Two stylized Ethiopian friends passing a glowing gold bingo ball between their hands,
> linked by a mint light ribbon that curls into a chain of small numbered balls. Deep
> ink-green background, woven tibeb strip at the bottom, warm rim light. Space at the top
> for a headline and at the bottom for a referral code chip. Flat vector, warm and human,
> faces turned three-quarters away from camera, no logos, no text inside the artwork.

Headline: `Bring a friend. Share the wins.` / `ጓደኛዎን ይጋብዙ። አብረው ያሸንፉ።`
Code chip: `bingo251.et · code {{CODE}}`

### 3.4 Live draw / jackpot poster (16:9 and 9:16)

> Dynamic poster of a live bingo draw: a glass drum of glowing numbered balls, one ball
> mid-air with a gold motion trail, concentric mint rings radiating from it. Dark ink
> background with faint card grids. Bold headline zone on the left third, vertical tibeb
> strip on the right edge. High contrast, flat vector with light grain, mint “LIVE” pill
> in the corner, 18+ badge bottom-right.

Headline: `Every 3 minutes. Live.` / `በየ3 ደቂቃው። ቀጥታ።`
Subline: “Watch the draw even when you are not playing.”

### 3.5 Agent recruitment poster (4:5)

> Poster aimed at local shop owners: a warm-lit kiosk counter at dusk, a phone showing a
> gold bingo ball, a hand offering credits to a customer. Deep green-black surroundings,
> gold practical lights, mint UI glow on the phone. Woven tibeb awning strip. Headline
> space at the top, CTA space at the bottom. Flat vector with textured shadows,
> aspirational and trustworthy, no cash, no logos.

Headline: `Become a Bingo 251 agent` / `የቢንጎ 251 ወኪል ይሁኑ`
Subline: “Serve your neighbourhood. Earn on every top-up.”

### 3.6 Weekly tournament poster (1:1)

> Scoreboard-inspired poster: a gold trophy formed from stacked bingo balls on a dark
> podium of card grids, mint confetti arcs, tibeb ribbon wrapping the podium. Big
> headline area up top, prize-pool chip at the bottom, 18+ mark in the corner. Flat
> vector, celebratory, restrained palette.

Headline: `Saturday Night Bingo` / `የቅዳሜ ምሽት ቢንጎ`
Subline: `Prize pool {{AMOUNT}} credits`

## 4. Telegram-specific assets

- **Bot avatar / Mini App icon:** use §2.1 at 512×512 PNG.
- **Story sticker / share card:** §3.1 at 1080×1920 with the bottom 320px kept free for
  Telegram’s reply bar.
- **Announcement banner inside the app:** 3:1 (1500×500), artwork on the right 40%,
  empty dark space on the left for the `title`/`body` that the admin enters in
  **Admin → Marketing**. Text-free artwork only.
- **Popup artwork:** 16:9 (1200×675), centered object, dark vignette edges, no text.

## 5. Production notes

1. Generate at the largest size available, then downscale; keep one master per poster.
2. Ask for “no text” and set the headline in the app or in Figma — generated lettering
   breaks Amharic glyphs.
3. Keep the 18+ and “Play responsibly” marks as separate overlays so compliance can be
   updated without regenerating art.
4. Export WebP for the app (`public/`), PNG for print, and keep the seed + prompt in this
   file so the set can be regenerated consistently.
