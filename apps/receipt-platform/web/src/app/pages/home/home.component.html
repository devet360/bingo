TRUST MODEL

A receipt is evidence.
It is not the authority.

Provider outputs are parsed locally, checked against the requested reference and expectations, and recorded with provenance. Screenshots alone are never enough to return VERIFIED. If a bank or relay is unavailable, the result stays explicit and reviewable.
